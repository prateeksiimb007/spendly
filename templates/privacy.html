Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects minimal data necessary to provide our personal expense tracking service. This includes:

  • Account information (email, password hash)
  • Expense entries (amount, category, date, description)
  • Financial summaries and reports you generate
  • App usage data (features accessed, interaction patterns)

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and maintain the Spendly expense tracking service
  • Generate expense reports and financial summaries
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Send important account notifications

We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on servers located in India. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

You can export your expense data at any time through your account, and you may delete your account and associated data by following the account deletion procedure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services to help provide and improve our service (such as hosting, analytics, and authentication services). These services have their own privacy policies and are contractually bound to protect your data.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Privacy Policy or data handling practices, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@spendly.app
Support: support@spendly.app

We will respond to privacy-related inquiries within 30 days.
